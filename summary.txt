## Taieb Hammami — Academic & Technical Profile

Taieb Hammami is a Tunisian **AI and Software Developer** currently pursuing a **Master’s degree in Information Systems and New Technologies (Master de Recherche – Systèmes d’Information et Nouvelles Technologies)**.

He previously obtained a **Master’s degree in Data Science and Engineering (SIDATA)** and a **Bachelor’s degree in Business Information Systems, specializing in Business Intelligence**.

### Main AI Project

His Master's project focused on an **AI-powered Cheque Reader**, combining computer vision, OCR, deep learning, and mobile development.

The system uses technologies such as **YOLO, Roboflow, TrOCR, CNN/LSTM/CTC, Flutter, Flask and OpenCV** to detect cheque fields and recognize handwritten amounts.
